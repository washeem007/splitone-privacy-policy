SplitOne — Privacy Policy

SplitOne ("the app") is an offline-first expense-splitting and trip-budgeting application. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as importantly, what it does not.

Summary

SplitOne is designed to work entirely offline. All the expenses, people, sessions, and trip data you create are stored only on your own device, in your browser/app's local storage. We do not operate a server that stores or has access to your expense data, and we do not use analytics or advertising services of any kind.

Data stored on your device

All app data session names, people you add, expenses, splits, sub-groups, squad templates, trip budgets, group fund records, and your theme preference — is stored locally using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (for example, using the app's "Export CSV" or "Copy Summary" features, which use your device's own share/clipboard functions).

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app's storage through your device settings, permanently deletes this data. We have no way to recover it, since we never had a copy of it.

Purchases (SplitOne Pro)

If you choose to purchase SplitOne Pro, the purchase itself is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other payment information — that is handled by Google, under Google's own privacy policy.

To confirm your purchase is valid, the app sends a purchase token (a reference ID generated by Google Play, not your personal or payment information) to a small verification service we operate. That service asks Google's Play Developer API whether the token corresponds to a genuine, completed purchase, and returns a simple yes/no answer to the app. This service does not store your purchase token, personal information, or any other data about you beyond what is necessary to complete that one check.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information — SplitOne does not require an account to use.
  • We do not use advertising networks, and the app shows no ads.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data about your usage of the app to begin with.

Children's privacy

SplitOne is not directed at children and is not designed to appeal primarily to children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, consistent with the fact that we do not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if we add a feature that changes how data is handled — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.
