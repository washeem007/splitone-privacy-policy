SplitOne

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

SplitOne works offline. The people, expenses, trip budgets and receipt photos you add stay on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. The only time the app talks to a server of ours is to confirm a Pro purchase.

This page explains what the app does with data, what it doesn’t do, and how to delete everything. It applies to SplitOne Lite and SplitOne Pro on Android.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your device:

  • session names, people, sub-groups and squad templates
  • expenses, splits, settlements, trip days, budgets and group fund records
  • receipt photos (Pro), in the app’s private storage
  • your settings, such as your theme

SplitOne switches off Android’s automatic cloud backup for its data, so nothing is copied to Google Drive or any other cloud service in the background. We never receive a copy, which also means we can’t recover your data if you lose it.

What leaves your phone

Only three things ever cross the boundary of the app, and only for the reasons below.

  • You buy Pro, or the app checks your Pro purchase

    Sent: a product ID and a purchase token issued by Google Play. No name, email or device ID.

    Goes to: our verification service on Google Cloud (Belgium), which confirms it with Google Play. More under “Purchases” below.

  • You copy a summary, tap Remind, or export a PDF or Excel file

    Sent: the text or file you chose.

    Goes to: wherever you send it, through Android’s share menu or clipboard. We never see it. Once you send it, the app you chose handles it under its own policy.

  • You tap the Privacy Policy link

    Sent: your phone’s browser opens this page.

    Goes to: GitHub Pages, which hosts this page and may log visits under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Exported PDF and Excel files are saved briefly in the app’s private storage so Android can share them. They stay there until Android clears the cache or you clear the app’s storage.

Purchases (SplitOne Pro)

Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details, billing address or any other payment information. Google’s handling of that is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy, and Google’s billing library may collect data of its own as described there.

To unlock Pro, the app sends the product ID and purchase token to a small verification service we run on Google Cloud in Belgium. It asks Google Play whether the purchase is genuine, completes the purchase confirmation with Google if that is still pending, and replies yes or no. This happens when you buy Pro, and when the app starts and Google Play shows you own Pro but the app hasn’t recorded it yet, for example after a reinstall. Until a check succeeds, the token is kept on your device so the app can try again.

The service has no database and keeps no record of you. Like most hosted services, Google Cloud keeps standard request logs, such as the time, IP address and device type of a request, for a limited period for security and troubleshooting. We do this to deliver the purchase you made and to prevent fraud.

Photos and camera

SplitOne doesn’t ask for camera or storage permission. When you attach a receipt photo (Pro), Android’s own camera or photo picker opens, and the app receives only the picture you choose. It shrinks the image, saves it in private storage, and deletes it when you remove the photo or delete its expense, day or session.

What we don’t collect

SplitOne has no account system, ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools. It doesn’t use your location, contacts or advertising ID. We don’t build profiles, and we don’t sell or share data about you.

All fonts and icons are built into the app, so opening it makes no requests to third parties.

Your choices and rights

To delete your data, delete sessions inside the app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall it. Any attached photos are removed with it.

Laws such as the GDPR and UK GDPR let you ask to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because your SplitOne data lives only on your phone, there is nothing for us to hand over. The only thing we might hold is the standard server logs described above. If you want to ask about those, or anything else, contact us.

Children

SplitOne isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data handling changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
